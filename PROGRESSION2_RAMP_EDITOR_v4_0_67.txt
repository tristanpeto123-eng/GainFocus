Gain Focus v4.0.67

NEW
- Progression 2 now has an adjustable ramp from Week 1 capacity to the full programme.
- Ramp duration: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 16 weeks.
- Ramp shape:
  * Conservative (slower early)
  * Even / linear
  * Fast start
  * Custom weekly working-set targets
- Ramp can be paused and resumed.
- Full programme volume remains tied to the actual end-goal programme.
- Full-volume target date is calculated separately from the bodyweight/physique end-goal date.
- Settings stores the chosen ramp and revision history.
- The automatic set progression now obeys the selected curve/custom weekly targets.

WHERE
- Train -> Two-stage progression -> Progression 2 -> Adjust Progression 2 ramp
- Account -> Settings -> Progression 2 ramp -> Adjust ramp
