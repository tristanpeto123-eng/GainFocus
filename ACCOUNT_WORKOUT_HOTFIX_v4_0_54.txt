ROOT CAUSE OF START/RESUME FAILURE
The v4.0.52 workout experience called `gf48IsCompound` from the older v4.0.48
module. That helper was private inside its module and had never been exported to
the global scope. The new Priority Lift calculation therefore raised a
ReferenceError as the session/live workout screen rendered.

v4.0.54 explicitly exports a safe equivalent helper and replaces Start/Resume
with a guarded launch path.

REBOOT VS RESET
Reboot = reload app, keep all data.
Reset/Delete = destructive and remains a separate action.
