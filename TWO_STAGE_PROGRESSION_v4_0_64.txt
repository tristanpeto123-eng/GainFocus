GAIN FOCUS PWA v4.0.64 — TWO-STAGE PROGRESSION GOALS

PROGRESSION 1
Now -> Week 1 capacity
- Training-readiness goal, not a physique/weight goal.
- Compares current weekly working sets with the Week-1 starting-plan target.
- Compares current workout frequency with Week-1 frequency.
- Shows current RIR against the Week-1 target effort.
- Reaches 100% once every Week-1 session has been completed at least once.
- Current onboarding readiness is used as the initial percentage.

PROGRESSION 2
Week 1 -> End goal
- Starts from the Week-1 programme baseline.
- Tracks Week-1 programme volume toward full target programme volume.
- Tracks bodyweight toward the final target separately.
- Existing v4.0.63 volume-ramp logic is explicitly assigned to this second progression.

UI
- Onboarding Build Programme step now shows two separate progression cards.
- Train page now gets a collapsible Two-stage progression widget.
- The two goals are saved into plan.training.progressionGoals.
- Progress percentages are recorded under progress.phaseProgress after workouts.

IMPORTANT
The two phases are intentionally different:
1. "Can I physically execute Week 1 as prescribed?"
2. "From that proven starting point, am I progressing toward the full training and physique goal?"
