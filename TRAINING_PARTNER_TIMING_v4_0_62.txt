GAIN FOCUS PWA v4.0.62 — TRAINING PARTNER TIMING

NEW
[PASS] Planner now distinguishes solo training from training with partner(s).
[PASS] Select 1, 2, 3 or 4 people training together.
[PASS] Select average swap / load-change time: 10–45 seconds.
[PASS] Default partner behaviour is alternate / round-robin sets on the same exercise.
[PASS] Partner sets fill part of prescribed rest instead of simply multiplying the entire workout duration.
[PASS] Session estimates, weekly estimates, overflow logic and Impact Report all use the partner-adjusted wall-clock time.
[PASS] Exercise reordering from v4.0.61 still recalculates timing after the new order.
[PASS] Training setup is stored in both plan.training and progress.settings.

TIME MODEL
For solo:
  exercise time = lifting + prescribed rest + station transition

For 2+ people alternating:
  - everyone performs each planned set
  - the other person's lifting occupies part of your rest interval
  - extra handoff/load-change time is included
  - if partner rotation is shorter than the prescribed rest, the remaining rest is still retained

This is intended to estimate real wall-clock session duration, not total person-minutes.

EXAMPLE
2 people · 4 sets · ~40 sec lifting · 120 sec prescribed rest · 20 sec handoff:
partner rotation fills much of the 120 sec rest, so the workout grows moderately rather
than being doubled.
